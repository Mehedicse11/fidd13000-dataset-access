🔒 Gated-access · Research only

FIDD-13000 Dataset

Large-scale benchmark for social media image forgery detection & localisation.
Request access for non‑commercial academic research, forensics, and fair evaluation.

📁 Code, weights & scripts: https://github.com/Mehedicse11/FIDF_FakeImageDetectionFramework

🎯 Who may apply

Academic / non-profit / governmental forensic agencies. Non‑commercial scientific research, education, or forensic tooling.
❌ Commercial entities or personal email addresses are not granted.

📬 How to request

Send email to mehediranacse11@gmail.com
Subject: FIDD-13000 Access Request — [Your Institution]
From a verifiable institutional email (.edu, .org, gov). Students must CC supervisor.

⏱️ After approval

Within 7 days: time‑limited download link + SHA‑256 checksum + README. Archive expires in 14 days. You’ll be added to the access‑holder list for updates & takedown notices.

📋 Required information (use template in §6)

Your request email must include all fields below — incomplete requests get one reply, then closed.

  • Full name of requester
  • Institutional affiliation (dept & univ)
  • Position (PhD, postdoc, faculty, analyst)
  • Supervisor (if student) + CC them
  • Institutional webpage / ORCID
  • Country of institution
  • Co‑investigators (if any)
  • Intended use (100–300 words)
  • Explicit DUA acceptance (verbatim)
⚠️ PhD / Master's students: must name supervisor and include them in CC line — requests without CC will not be processed.

📜 Data Use Agreement (DUA)

By requesting and downloading FIDD-13000, you agree to the following binding terms for yourself, co‑investigators, and institution.

✅ Permitted uses
  • Non‑commercial academic research in image/multimedia forensics, digital investigation
  • Training, evaluation, benchmarking of forgery detection & localisation methods
  • Aggregate results + small illustrative examples in academic publications (cite paper)
  • Classroom teaching at your institution (no redistribution to outsiders)
🚫 Prohibited uses
  • Redistribution to any third party (others must request individually)
  • Commercial use (products, services, consultancy)
  • Training generative models (GANs, diffusion, autoencoders, face synthesis)
  • Re‑identification, profiling, surveillance, or targeting individuals depicted
  • Operational content moderation / law enforcement pipelines without ethical review
  • Creating non‑consensual synthetic media
  • Bulk public display of images without written permission
🔐 Security & handling
  • Store on institution‑controlled systems, NOT personal cloud drives
  • Requester responsible for all co‑investigators’ compliance
  • If co‑investigator leaves group → delete local dataset copy
⚠️ Takedown & termination — Individuals can request removal; all DUA holders will be notified and must delete affected images and derived artefacts within 30 days. Violation may lead to immediate revocation and deletion requirements.
Citation required — Any publication using FIDD-13000 must cite:
M. M. R. Rana, M. A. Rahman, K. H. Talukder, R. Amin, "FIDF: A Multi-View Discrepancy-Gated Hypergraph Framework...", Forensic Science International: Digital Investigation, [DOI].

🕊️ Takedown procedure

Any individual who recognises themselves in FIDD-13000 (or authorised representative) may request removal. Email mehediranacse11@gmail.com with subject FIDD-13000 Takedown Request. Requests are acknowledged within 7 days and honoured within 30 days. All DUA holders will be notified and must delete affected images + derived artefacts.

📌 After submission

✔️ Approved: download link, SHA-256, changelog, DUA confirmation.
❓ Clarification needed: missing fields.
❌ Declined: commercial intent, non-institutional email, insufficient use description.

Access records (name, institution, country, approval date) maintained only for notifications & journal verification. No personal data retained beyond that.
